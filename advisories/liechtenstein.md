Exercise normal precaution

in **Liechtenstein**.

Liechtenstein is generally a safe destination for travelers.

  * Liechtenstein’s mountains can be dangerous because of avalanches, landslides, and sudden weather changes.
  * Review our [Travel Guidance for Liechtenstein](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/Liechtenstein.html#requirements "Travel Guidance") to learn how to prepare for a safe trip.
