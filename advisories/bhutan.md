Exercise normal precaution

in **Bhutan**.

Bhutan is generally a safe destination for travelers. Medical services for routine and emergency procedures are limited, specifically in rural areas. Read the Health section of the Travel Guidance on this page for more information.  

Bhutan regularly experiences flooding, landslides, and wildfires. The country is also vulnerable to earthquakes. Review our information on Weather and Natural Disasters in Bhutan.  

Review our [Travel Guidance](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/bhutan.html#requirements "Travel Guidane") for Bhutan to learn how to prepare for a safe trip.
