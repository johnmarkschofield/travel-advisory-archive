**Exercise increased caution**  in **Cambodia** due to **crime** , **unrest** , and **unexploded ordnance**. Some areas have increased risk. Read the entire Travel Advisory.

  * **Exercise increased caution** in **Phnom Penh** due to **crime**.
  * **Exercise Increased Caution** in **Battambang** , **Banteay Meanchey** , **Pursat** , **Siem Reap** , **Pailin** , **Kampong Thom and Preah Vihear provinces** due to **unexploded ordnance**.
