# Gaza

**Level 4 Advisory**
**Date:** Wed, 26 Aug 2026
**Source:** [State.gov Link](https://travel.state.gov/content/tsg_aem/us/en/home/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.xgz.html)

---

Do not travel

to **Gaza** due to **terrorism** , **unrest** , and **limited health infrastructure.**

**Restrictions on U.S. Government Personnel Movement**

U.S. government employees are prohibited from traveling to Gaza for any reason due to safety risks. The U.S. government is unable to provide routine or emergency consular services to U.S. citizens in Gaza.

**Terrorism**

Terrorist groups, lone-actor terrorists, and other violent extremists continue plotting possible attacks in Gaza. Terrorists and violent extremists may attack with little or no warning, targeting tourist locations, transportation hubs, markets, shopping malls, and local government facilities. The security environment is complex and can change quickly.

There is risk of terrorist violence, including terrorist attacks and other activity in Gaza. Visit the U.S. Department of State's [country reports on terrorism](https://www.state.gov/country-reports-on-terrorism/) to learn more.

**Unrest**

The security environment within Gaza and on its borders is extremely dangerous and volatile.

If you are a U.S. citizen or Lawful Permanent Resident (LPR) that needs our assistance, and you have not already provided your information to the Department of State, please email U.S. Embassy Jerusalem at [JerusalemACS@state.gov](mailto:JerusalemACS@state.gov).

**Health**

Emergency and intensive care facilities are limited in Gaza. Hospitals and clinics lack basic resources.
