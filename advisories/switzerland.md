Exercise normal precaution

in **Switzerland.**

Switzerland is generally a safe destination for travelers.

Switzerland's mountains can be dangerous because of avalanches, landslides, and sudden weather changes. Review our [Travel Guidance for Switzerland](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/switzerland.html#requirements "Travel Guidance for Switzerland") to learn how to prepare for a safe trip.
