**Exercise increased caution** in **Uruguay** due to **crime**.

Crime is most common in these departments:

  * Montevideo
  * Canelones
  * Maldonado
  * Rivera



Violent crime occurs across the country. Crimes include homicide, armed robbery, carjacking and theft. These crimes can happen at any time and have affected U.S. citizens.

Criminals often travel in pairs on motorcycles. They may approach victims with a weapon and demand personal belongings. Armed criminals also target grocery stores, restaurants, financial centers, and small businesses. Sometimes innocent bystanders become victims. Review our [Travel Guidance for Uruguay](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/uruguay.html#requirements "Travel Guidance for Uruguay") to learn how to prepare for a safe trip.
