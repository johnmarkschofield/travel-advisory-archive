Exercise increased caution

in the **United Kingdom** due to **terrorism**.

There is risk of terrorist violence, including terrorist attacks and other activity in the United Kingdom.**** Visit the U.S. Department of State's [country reports on terrorism](https://www.state.gov/country-reports-on-terrorism/) to learn more.

There is also risk of isolated violence by dissident groups in Northern Ireland. These groups mainly target police and military.

Petty crime is common, especially in popular tourist locations. Stay aware of your surroundings. Most crimes against foreigners are crimes of opportunity, like purse or cellphone snatching and pickpocketing.
