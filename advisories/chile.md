Exercise increased caution

in **Chile  due to crime, unrest, and health.**

##### **Crime**

  * Petty crime is common in Chile. Common crimes include mugging, pickpocketing, and theft. Violent crimes also occur, including assaults, homicide, carjackings, and residential break-ins.



##### **Unrest**

Large-scale demonstrations may occur in Santiago and other cities in Chile.

  * Demonstrations can take place with little or no notice.
  * Demonstrations often result in disruptions to transportation, including public buses and Santiago metro services.



##### **Health**

The U.S. Centers for Disease Control (CDC) has issued a [Travelers’ Health Notice for Andes virus (Hantavirus)](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/notices "CDC Travel Health Notices") in the region.

  * The risk for most travelers is extremely low.
