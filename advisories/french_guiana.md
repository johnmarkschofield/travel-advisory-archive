# French Guiana

**Level 1 Advisory**
**Date:** Wed, 08 Jul 2026
**Source:** [State.gov Link](https://travel.state.gov/content/tsg_aem/us/en/home/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.guf.html)

Exercise normal precaution

in French Guiana.

French Guiana is generally a safe destination for travelers.

There is no official U.S. consular representation in French Guiana.  Consular services are provided by the [U.S. Embassy in Paramaribo, Suriname](https://sr.usembassy.gov/).

Review our [Travel Guidance](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/french-guiana.html#requirements) for French Guiana to learn how to prepare for a safe trip.
