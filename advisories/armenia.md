# Armenia

**Level 2 Advisory**
**Date:** Fri, 05 Sep 2025
**Source:** [State.gov Link](https://travel.state.gov/content/tsg_aem/us/en/home/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.arm.html)

---

Exercise increased caution

in **Armenia** due to areas of **potential armed conflict**. Some areas have increased risk. Read the entire Travel Advisory.

U.S. citizens should exercise increased caution in Armenia. Further military activity could occur in the region.  

  * Read the [Travel Guidance for Armenia](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/armenia.html#requirements "Travel Guidance for Armenia") for additional information on travel to Armenia.



**Do Not Travel** To These Areas for Any Reason:

  * The border region with Azerbaijan.



**U.S. Embassy employees and their families cannot travel to these areas for non-essential reasons:**

  * Gegharkunik region east of Vardenis
  * Syunik region east of Goris
  * Syunik region south of Kapan



Travel through Yeraskh village in Ararat region is allowed, stopping is not.
