# Guyana

**Level 3 Advisory**
**Date:** Wed, 26 Aug 2026
**Source:** [State.gov Link](https://travel.state.gov/content/tsg_aem/us/en/home/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.guy.html)

---

Reconsider travel

to **Guyana** due to **crime**.

Violent crime is common, especially at night. Crimes include murder and armed robbery. Local police often lack the resources to respond effectively to serious criminal incidents. 

  * Stabroek Market is off limits to U.S. Government employees at all times due to crime. 
  * Bourda Market is off limits to U.S. Government employees during evening hours due to crime. 
  * Avoid National Park and Seawall after dark due to vehicle break-ins, poor lighting, and high pedestrian and vehicular traffic.
  * Exercise caution in Agricola, Albouystown, and Tiger Bay due to high crime levels.
  * Review the [Travel Guidance](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/guyana.html#requirements "Guyana Travel Guidance") in Guyana for additional information.
