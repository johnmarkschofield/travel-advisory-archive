# Guyana

**Level 3 Advisory**
**Date:** Mon, 12 May 2025
**Source:** [State.gov Link](https://travel.state.gov/content/tsg_aem/us/en/home/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.guy.html)

---

Reconsider travel

to **Guyana** due to **crime**.

Violent crime, including murder and armed robbery, is common, especially at night. Local police often lack the resources to respond effectively to serious criminal incidents. 

Review the [Travel Guidance](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/guyana.html#requirements "Guyana Travel Guidance") in Guyana for additional information.
