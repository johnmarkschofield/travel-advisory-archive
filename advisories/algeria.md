**There were no changes to the advisory level or risk indicators. Advisory summary was updated.**

Exercise increased caution in Algeria due to **terrorism** and **kidnapping**. Some areas have increased risk. Read the entire Travel Advisory.

**Do Not Travel to:**

  * Areas near the eastern, and southern borders, Bordj Badji Mokhtar, Illiz, In Guezzsam, In Salah, Tamanrasset, the mountainous areas of the Wilaya of Tebessa, and Tindouf due to **terrorism** and**kidnapping.**
  * Remote areas in the Sahara Desert without the support of an organized tour group due to **terrorism** and **kidnapping**.  
  




**Advisory summary**

Terrorism remains a possible threat in Algeria. Terrorist attacks have historically targeted Algerian security forces. Attacks are possible in both rural and urban areas despite a heavy and active police presence. Kidnapping for ransom events in Algeria's deep south have targeted foreigners in recent years.

The U.S. government has limited ability to offer emergency services to U.S. citizens outside Algiers province. This is due to the size of the country, as well as the Algerian government's travel restrictions on U.S. government employees.

**Terrorism**

There is risk of terrorist violence, including terrorist attacks and other activity in Algeria. Visit the U.S. Department of State's [country reports](https://www.state.gov/reports/country-reports-on-terrorism-2023/algeria/) on terrorism to learn more.

Review our information on [Terrorism](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/global-events/terrorism.html) and [Travel to High-Risk Areas.](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/high-risk-areas.html)

**Kidnapping and hostage taking**

Foreigners may be targeted for kidnapping or hostage taking by criminals and terrorists in Algeria. This is most likely to occur in the southern desert regions near the borders with Mali, Niger, or Libya and areas near migration networks.

**Level 4: Do Not Travel**

**Eastern and southern borders and other specific areas**

Avoid travel to rural areas within 37 miles (60 kilometers) of the Algerian borders with Libya, Niger, Mali, and Mauritania This is due to the threat of terrorist and criminal activities, including criminal kidnapping and terrorist hostage taking.  

In addition to these borders, do not travel to the following places for any reason:

  * Bordj Badji Mokhtar
  * Illizi
  * In Guezzsam
  * In Salah
  * Tamanrasset
  * Tebessa Wilaya mountainous areas
  * Tindouf



**Level 4: Do Not Travel**

**Overland travel to the Sahara Desert**  
Do not travel large distances overland in the Sahara Desert outside of an organized tour group, or cross Algeria's borders with Mali, Niger, or Libya due to terrorist and criminal activity, including criminal kidnapping and terrorist hostage taking. Consular staff and first responders likely will not be able to reach you in the event of an emergency. Cellular coverage is unreliable outside of major cities.

  * Stay aware of your surroundings.
