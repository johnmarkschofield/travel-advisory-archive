Exercise normal precaution

in **Montserrat**.

Montserrat is generally a safe destination for travelers.

Montserrat regularly experiences hurricanes. Review our information on [Weather and Natural Disasters](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/montserrat.html#weather "Montserrat Weather and Natural Disasters") in Montserrat.

##### **Soufrière Hills volcano**

The Soufrière Hills volcano has been active since 1995. The last major volcanic activity occurred in 2010. There are 5 exclusion zones around the volcano. Visitors must obtain special permission to enter the most restricted zone in southern Montserrat.  Do not enter these zones without a special permit.
