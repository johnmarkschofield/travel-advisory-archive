# Kosovo

**Level 2 Advisory**
**Date:** Tue, 25 Aug 2026
**Source:** [State.gov Link](https://travel.state.gov/content/tsg_aem/us/en/home/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.xks.html)

---

Exercise increased caution

in **Kosovo** due to **unrest** and**terrorism.**

##### Unrest

Demonstrations related to the local political situation may occur with little notice.  They may disrupt transportation and other essential services.

##### Terrorism

There is risk of terrorist violence, including terrorist attacks and other activity in the Balkans region, including Kosovo.  Visit the U.S. Department of State's Kosovo country reports on terrorism to learn more.

**Reconsider travel** to **North Kosovo area** due to risk of **unrest.**
