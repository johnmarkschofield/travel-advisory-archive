Exercise increased caution

in the **Philippines** due to **crime** , **terrorism** , **unrest** , and **kidnapping**. Some areas have increased risk. Read the entire Travel Advisory. 

**Exercise increased caution** due to **terrorism** and **unrest** associated with ongoing insurgent activities in certain rural and remote regions of **Leyte** , **Mindoro** , **Negros** , and **Samar**.

  * Foreign terrorist organizations actively recruit Americans to participate directly in terrorist activities and/or provide financial support.
  * Terrorists and armed groups have carried out kidnappings, bombings, and other attacks. They have targeted public areas such as tourist sites, markets, and local government facilities in the Philippines. 
  * Violent crimes are common. They include robberies, kidnappings, physical assaults, and homicides. 
  * Protests can turn violent or result in traffic jams and road closures. Local governments have limited capacity to respond. 
  * The Philippines regularly experiences typhoons, earthquakes, and volcanic eruptions. Review our information on [Weather and Natural Disasters](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/philippines.html#weather "Philippines Weather and Natural Disasters"). 



##### **Terrorism**

There is risk of terrorist violence, including terrorist attacks and other activity, in the Philippines. Visit the U.S. Department of State's [Country Reports on Terrorism](https://www.state.gov/country-reports-on-terrorism-2 "Country Reports on Terrorism") to learn more. 

**Do Not Travel to:**

  * The Sulu Archipelago, including the southern Sulu Sea, due to the risk of **crime** , **terrorism** , **unrest** , and **kidnapping**.



  * Marawi City in Mindanao due to the risk of **terrorism** and **unrest**.




**Reconsider Travel to:  **

  * Certain other areas of Mindanao (see below) due to **crime** , **terrorism** , **unrest** , and **kidnapping**.
