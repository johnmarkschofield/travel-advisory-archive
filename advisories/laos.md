Exercise increased caution

in **Laos** due to **crime** , **unrest** , and **unexploded ordnances**. Some areas have increased risk. Review the entire Travel Advisory.

Review our [Travel Guidance](/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/laos.html "Laos Trael Guidance") for Laos to learn how to prepare for a safe trip.

  * **Reconsider Travel** to **Xaisomboun Province** due to **civil unrest.**
  * **Exercise Increased Caution** in areas on the **Border with Burma** , **Savannakhet,** **Xieng Khouang** , **Salavan** , **Khammouane** , **Sekong** , **Champasak** , **Houaphanh** , **Attapeu** , **Luang Prabang** , and **Vientiane provinces** , along **Route 7 (from Route 13 to the Vietnam border), Route 9 (Savannakhet to the Vietnam border)** , and **Route 20 (Pakse to Salavan)** due to **crime**.
  * **Exercise Increased Caution** in areas of **Savannakhet, Xieng Khouang, Salavan, Khammouane, Sekong, Champasak, Houaphanh, Attapeu, Luang Prabang** , and **Vientiane provinces** , as well as **along Route 7** due to **unexploded ordnances**.
