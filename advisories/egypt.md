# Egypt

**Level 2 Advisory**
**Date:** Tue, 24 Nov 2026
**Source:** [State.gov Link](https://travel.state.gov/content/tsg_aem/us/en/home/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.egy.html)

---

Exercise increased caution

in **Egypt**  due to **terrorism** , **crime** , and **health,  landmines, **and **limited medical infrastructure in rural areas.** Some areas have increased risk. Read the entire Travel Advisory.

##### Crime

  * Most crimes against foreigners are crimes of opportunity, like purse snatching and pickpocketing. Harassment of women, including foreigners, is a problem in Egypt. It can include vulgar comments, gestures, indecent exposure, and unwanted physical contact. Tourists should beware of overcharging and scams in tourist areas. Review the Overseas Security Advisory Council’s (OSAC) [Country Security Report for Egypt](https://www.osac.gov/Content/Report/f9535564-b44b-40f6-971b-1c31033d9a55) for further details.  



##### Terrorism

  * There is risk of terrorist violence, including terrorist attacks and other activity in Egypt, particularly in Central and Northern Central Sinai Peninsula and along the Southern and Western borders. 
  * Visit the U.S. Department of State's [country reports on terrorism](https://www.state.gov/country-reports-on-terrorism/) to learn more.



##### Health

  * Emergency and intensive care facilities are limited in Egypt and may be unavailable in remote areas. Ambulances are rare, unreliable in most areas, and don’t have advanced medical equipment. 



##### Landmines

  * There are World War II-era unexploded landmines in these areas: 
    * The Sinai Peninsula 
    * Mediterranean coast west of Alexandria 
    * Eastern Desert between Cairo and the Suez Canal 
  * Pay attention to warning signs in these areas. 



**Limited consular assistance for dual nationals**

  * The U.S. Embassy has limited ability to help dual national U.S.-Egyptian citizens who are arrested or detained.
  * Egyptian authorities regard dual citizens as Egyptian citizens, which can limit or hinder some forms of assistance.



**White and Black Desert**

  * These areas are remote with a likely delayed emergency response.
  * U.S. government employees must travel with licensed tour groups if going to any of the following locations:
    * White Desert
    * Black Desert



**Americans should reconsider travel to:**

  * Western and Libyan Deserts due to terrorism and delayed emergency response.



**Americans should not travel to:**

  * The Libyan and Sudan border regions including the Hala’ib Triangle __ due to terrorism and delayed emergency response.
  * Central and Northern Sinai due to due to terrorism and delayed emergency response.
